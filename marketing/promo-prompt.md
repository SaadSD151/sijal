- كل مشهد سوّه مرتين: مرة بمقاس الجوال 9:16 (تيك توك وانستا)، ومرة بمقاس 16:9 بدقة 1080p (يوتيوب).

## التعليمات العامة (الصقها أول شي)

```
I am making a 40-second promotional trailer for "Sijal" (سِجال), a Saudi-made real-time strategy war game played in the browser on mobile and PC. I will ask you for 5 separate 8-second clips. Keep the same style in every clip:

- Look: a painted top-down world map with colourful nations spreading over land, gold and deep navy colours, epic and cinematic, smooth camera moves over the map. Match the attached game screenshots as closely as possible.
- Voice: ONE deep, confident Saudi male narrator speaking in Saudi (Najdi/Hijazi) dialect Arabic, energetic like a gaming trailer. Speak ONLY the Arabic line I give, word for word, clearly.
- Sound: epic war drums and orchestral music under the voice, with battle and explosion effects. No other voices, no background talking.
- No text on screen unless I ask for it. Do not invent other logos or brand names.
- Aspect ratio: [9:16 vertical] (or [16:9, 1080p] for the YouTube version).
```
